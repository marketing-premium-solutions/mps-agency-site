=== SECTION 9 — FAQ (elementskit-accordion, 6) ===
Eyebrow: • Questions
H2: SEO Questions, Answered
Q1 How long does SEO actually take? | Local rankings often move within 2 to 3 months. Competitive terms take 4 to 6 months to reach the top of page one, and 9 to 12 months to truly dominate. Anyone promising page one in 30 days is either guessing or using tactics that get sites penalised. We set realistic timelines upfront and report progress every month.
Q2 What is the difference between SEO and Google Ads? | Google Ads buys instant visibility and stops the moment you stop paying. SEO builds organic rankings that compound and keep working. Ads are the fastest way to start. SEO lowers your long-term cost per lead. Most growing businesses run Ads first for immediate leads, then layer SEO so they are not renting all their traffic forever.
Q3 What is keyword fan-out and why does it matter? | One seed keyword, "greenhouse tunnels", fans out into dozens of searches: "greenhouse tunnel installation Pretoria", "how much does a greenhouse tunnel cost", "greenhouse tunnel repair near me". Each is a different person at a different stage of buying. We build a page for each one instead of cramming them all onto one page.
Q4 Can you guarantee rankings? | No honest SEO can guarantee a specific position, because Google controls the algorithm. Anyone who guarantees page one is a red flag. What we guarantee is real strategy, transparent reporting and steady, compounding progress on the keywords that bring you customers.
Q5 How are you different from cheaper agencies? | Cheap packages usually mean spam backlinks and automated reports with no real action, which can get your site penalised. We do manual, white-hat work: real technical fixes, genuine content and quality South African backlinks built by outreach. You see exactly what we do each month, and you own every asset.
Q6 What happens if I cancel? | You keep everything we built: the on-page work, the content, the technical fixes and the rankings they earned. Rankings may soften over time without ongoing work as competitors keep pushing, but nothing is taken away when you leave.
