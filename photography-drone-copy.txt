=== SECTION 9 — FAQ ===
Eyebrow: • Questions
H2: Photography and Drone Questions, Answered
Q1 Do I need to do anything before the shoot? | After booking we send a shot list and a simple prep guide: what areas to tidy, what to have ready, what to wear if team members will be in the photos. Most clients need about 30 minutes of preparation.
Q2 Can you fly a drone anywhere? | Not everywhere. We operate under CAA regulations. Restricted zones include within 10km of controlled aerodromes, certain urban areas, and above people without permits. We check your location before confirming a drone shoot and handle permit applications where needed.
Q3 How many photos will we get? | A half-day ground shoot typically delivers 60 to 100 edited images. A full day delivers 150 or more. Quality is the priority: we don't pad the delivery with near-identical shots.
Q4 How long does editing take? | Standard turnaround is 5 to 7 working days. Rush delivery (48 hours) is available as an add-on.
Q5 Can we use the photos in our Google Ads and on social media? | Yes. You own all files outright and can use them in any channel, at any time, without any licence fee or restriction. We recommend the WebP versions for web and ads, and the JPEG originals for print.
Q6 What if the weather is bad on the day? | We monitor weather before every shoot. For drone shoots, wind above 40km/h or rain means we reschedule at no charge. For ground shoots, overcast is usually fine. We'll contact you the evening before if we need to move the date.
