=== SECTION 9 — FAQ ===
Eyebrow: • Questions
H2: TikTok Questions, Answered
Q1 Do we need to be involved in the filming? | For the Starter package, you provide the footage. For Business and Growth, we handle the filming. You need to be available on shoot day (or have a team member there for context), but you don't need to be on camera unless you want to be.
Q2 What if TikTok doesn't work for our industry? | TikTok works for almost every industry when the content is right. The platform rewards useful, interesting or entertaining content, not just dance videos. We have run successful accounts for trade businesses, service companies and B2B brands. We do honest onboarding: if your industry genuinely isn't a good fit, we'll tell you before you sign.
Q3 How long before we see results? | Most accounts see meaningful reach growth within 60 to 90 days of consistent posting with a solid strategy. Follower growth follows reach. Patience and consistency are the only formula that works.
Q4 Do you post on other platforms too? | Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts are available as add-ons. We reformat the content for each platform rather than cross-posting the same file.
Q5 Who owns the content? | You do. Every video file is downloaded and stored in a shared Google Drive folder under your account. We post with access you grant us, and you can remove that access at any time.
Q6 What happens if we cancel? | No penalty, no cancellation fee. We wrap up at the end of the billing month, send you all the files and hand over access. Everything we built stays with you.
