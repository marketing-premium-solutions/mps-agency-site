=== SECTION 9 — FAQ (elementskit-accordion, 6) ===
Eyebrow: • Questions
H2: Local SEO Questions, Answered
Q1 What is the difference between Local SEO and regular SEO? | Regular SEO targets broad keywords nationally. Local SEO targets location-specific searches like "plumber in Centurion", "dentist near Fourways" or "electrician Midrand", and focuses on the map pack and local organic results. If your customers come from specific areas, local SEO delivers the fastest, most measurable return.
Q2 Do I actually need a dedicated page for every suburb I serve? | Yes, if you want to rank in each one. Google ranks pages, not businesses. To appear when someone in Krugersdorp searches for your service, you need a page that targets Krugersdorp, with localised content, the right keyword in the H1 and schema markup. An "Areas We Serve" list does not rank for anything.
Q3 Is Google Business Profile management included? | No. GBP setup and optimisation is a once-off add-on at R1,500. Ongoing GBP management is handled separately on request. We are upfront about this because hiding it inside a retainer is one of the oldest tricks in local SEO.
Q4 How many location pages do I need? | Start with the areas that bring in the most revenue. A plumber in Pretoria might start with Centurion, Pretoria East, Pretoria North, Midrand and Soshanguve: five pages, five new ranking opportunities. A few well-built pages always beat many thin ones, and we advise on scope based on your area, competition and budget.
Q5 How long before I start ranking locally? | Low-competition suburbs often move within 6 to 8 weeks of a page being indexed. Competitive areas like Sandton or Pretoria CBD take 3 to 6 months. We track each location separately and report monthly, so you always know where things stand.
Q6 Can location pages be added to my existing website? | Yes. They can be added to any WordPress site without a rebuild. We build them as new pages, connect them to your navigation and internal links, and submit them to Search Console.
