• Core Features

Everything a school needs to run

Built around how South African private and independent schools actually operate, not a repackaged system from overseas.

Digital Attendance Registers

Class teachers mark registers on any device in under two minutes. Absences are logged automatically and parents notified instantly. No paper. No end-of-day admin.

CAPS-Aligned Mark Books

Enter assessment results and the system calculates averages, weighting and promotion status automatically. Grade R to Grade 12, aligned to CAPS requirements.

Automated Report Cards

Generate ministry-compliant report cards for an entire class in one click. Marks pull through automatically. No copying, no formatting, no version control headaches.

Fee Tracking and Statements

Record payments, generate statements and see who is outstanding at a glance. Send automated reminders to parents for overdue accounts. All fees in one ledger.

Parent Portal

Parents log in to see their child's attendance, marks, fee balance and report cards in real time. Reduce inbound calls and give parents the visibility they expect.

Principal Dashboard

School-wide attendance, class averages, fee collection status and outstanding balances on one screen. Know what is happening across every grade without asking anyone.

• Transparency

What School Companion does not do

We would rather you know this upfront than discover it after sign-up.

Payroll and HR

School Companion manages student and academic records. Staff payroll and HR belong in a dedicated system.

Timetabling

We do not generate or manage school timetables. You bring your timetable; we operate within it.

Library management

Book borrowing, catalogue management and library systems are outside the scope of School Companion.

Online learning (LMS)

School Companion is a school management system, not a learning management system. It does not host lessons, assignments or course content.
