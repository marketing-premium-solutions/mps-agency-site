=== SECTION 9 — FAQ ===
Eyebrow: • Questions
H2: School Companion, Answered
Q1 How long does setup take? | Setting up our school management system takes just 5 working days for most schools. We handle the technical work: configuring your grades, subjects, staff accounts and fee structure. You simply provide the information.
Q2 Does it work during load shedding? | Yes. School Companion caches recent data so teachers can mark attendance offline. It syncs automatically when connectivity returns.
Q3 Can parents access the system? | Parents receive automated notifications for absences, results and fee statements. A parent portal is available on the School and Campus tiers.

Q5 What happens if we cancel? | Your data belongs to your school. On cancellation we export all records in standard formats and hand them over before your subscription ends.
Q6 Do you train our staff? | Yes. Onboarding includes a live training session for your admin team and a short video library for teachers. Ongoing support is via email or WhatsApp.
Q7 Can we run it alongside our current system? | Yes, during transition. School Companion is designed to replace paper registers, Excel spreadsheets and WhatsApp groups over time — we help you migrate.
